How the FEM Ecosystem Works

From problem formulation to measurable business outcomes—here is how entrepreneurs and mentors collaborate on FEM.

1

Submit Your Concrete Business Challenge

Instead of wading through hundreds of resumes, you articulate the exact issue blocking your business—whether cashflow timing, packaging durability, vendor negotiations, or channel distribution.

2

Intelligent Multi-Factor Matching

Our transparent matching engine evaluates:

  • Domain & Expertise Match (30%) — Specific functional capabilities required
  • Problem Similarity (30%) — Tangible problems previously resolved by the mentor
  • Industry Experience (15%) — Depth of relevant sector exposure
  • Business Stage (10%) — Early-stage, prototype, or growth alignment
  • Rural / Regional Focus (10%) — Hands-on experience with grassroots supply chains
  • Track Record (5%) — Verified outcomes logged on the platform
3

Review Transparent Match Explanations

Every matched mentor includes a natural-language explanation showing exactly why they were recommended. You can inspect their documented past case studies and request a focused 1-on-1 session.

4

Mentorship Sessions & Verified Impact Credits

Mentors review incoming requests, schedule diagnostic sessions, and log actionable action plans. Upon completion, verified Mentor Credits are awarded to reflect real hours and supported businesses.

5

Peer Collaboration & Community

Beyond mentorship, founders connect with other entrepreneurs to pool logistics, co-source raw materials, share transport routes, and exchange candid failure lessons.
