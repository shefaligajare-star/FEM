How Mentor Credits are Calculated

Mentor Credits incentivize real, impactful advisory work rather than passive presence. Scores cannot be manually edited by users. Points are mathematically weighted:

Credit Score = (Founders Mentored × 15) + (Mentoring Hours × 8) + (Rural Businesses × 25) + (Successful Outcomes × 35) + (Community Contributions × 5)

High-credit mentors receive preferential visibility in problem matching and recognized badges across the ecosystem.
