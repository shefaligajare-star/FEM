Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

Flourishing Entrepreneurs and Mentors (FEM) collects information necessary to match entrepreneurs with mentors and foster ecosystem collaboration. This includes registration details (name, email, city, region), business profiles, problem statements, and mentorship history.

2. Problem Matching and Transparency

Submitted business challenges are used strictly to run our problem-based matching engine. We calculate similarity scores against mentor profiles and experience records. Your contact details remain confidential until you explicitly send or accept a mentorship request.

3. Offline Data Storage

FEM utilizes browser IndexedDB and the Cache API to enable offline functionality. Data stored locally on your device (such as draft problems or queued messages) is encrypted by your browser's origin sandbox and transmitted to FEM servers only when network connectivity is available.

4. Data Protection & Security

We use industry-standard password hashing, CSRF protection, and secure server-side ownership validations to safeguard your account. We never sell user information to third-party advertisers.

5. Contacting Us

If you have questions regarding your data or wish to delete your account, visit your Account Settings or Contact Support.
