⚡ Built for Low & Intermittent Connectivity

An Offline-First Community Architecture

Entrepreneurs in remote or rural locations shouldn't be blocked by unreliable networks. Create posts, draft problems, message collaborators, and record insights even when completely disconnected. FEM saves everything locally in IndexedDB and automatically synchronizes when connectivity returns.

Service Worker Cache

App shell, dashboards, and static assets remain functional without connection.

IndexedDB Queue

Operations are queued safely on-device with zero data loss.

Conflict Resolution

Transactional batch sync automatically reconciles server and client state.
